# **Energy Field System: Alpha Rulebook**

## **Overview**

Energy Field is a deterministic, strategy-based collectible card game
set in a hard science fiction universe. Each card represents advanced
technologies, psionic disciplines, cybernetic augmentations, or quantum
devices. There are no random rolls or chance events --- all outcomes
follow clear cause-and-effect rules based on energy interactions and
system states.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## **1. Core Concepts**

### **1.1 Players and Systems**

Each player represents a combatant, pilot, AI, or adept controlling a
set of systems.\
Players begin the match with:

-   **6 Equipment Slots** --- spaces to install cards in *Equipment
    Mode*\
-   **10 Wound Slots** --- representing physical or systemic integrity\
-   **Energy Pool** --- stores available energy types (Kinetic, Field,
    Mental, etc.)

A player is defeated when all wound slots are filled.

### **1.2 Cards**

Each card has **two sides**:

-   **Action Side** --- a one-time or ongoing effect (attack, scan,
    heal, hack, etc.)
-   **Equipment Side** --- a permanent system that grants passive
    effects or defenses.

Flipping a card changes its role. You may flip once per turn during your
Equipment Phase.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## **2. Energy Domains**

Each card belongs to an **Energy Domain**.\
Domains define both attack and defense characteristics.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Domain           Defense Type          Field Effect          Counters
  ---------------- --------------------- --------------------- ---------------
  **Cybernetic**   Kinetic Armor         Weapon efficiency     Counters
                                                               Biotech

  **Digital**      Firewall              Program chaining      Counters
                                                               Quantum

  **Psionic**      Willpower Field       Mind-link chaining    Counters
                                                               Cybernetic

  **Quantum**      Phase Shift           Field superposition   Counters
                                                               Digital

  **Biotech**      Regeneration          Mutation growth       Counters
                                                               Psionic
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------

When two or more cards of the same domain are active, they create a
**Synergy Field** --- a shared passive benefit.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## **3. Equipment and Actions**

### **3.1 Equipment Mode**

-   Provides passive defenses or energy output.
-   May include one or more *defense types* (e.g., Kinetic +1).\
-   Contributes to a Synergy Field if other same-domain cards are
    active.

### **3.2 Action Mode**

-   Executes a specific ability.\
-   While active, the card provides **no defense** that turn.\
-   Flipping back to Equipment Mode restores defense next turn.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## **4. Combat and Wounds**

### **4.1 Wound Slots**

Damage is represented by filling **Wound Slots**.\
Each wound has a **severity level** depending on the attack's power.

  Severity   Description    Slot Fill
  ---------- -------------- -----------
  1          Minor          1
  3          Serious        3
  6          Critical       6
  10         Catastrophic   10

A player loses when all 10 wound slots are filled.

### **4.2 Defense Reduction**

Each defense layer reduces wound severity **one step**:

> 10 → 6 → 3 → 1 → 0

Defenses are domain-based. For example, Kinetic Armor defends against
Physical or Cybernetic attacks, while Phase Shift protects against
Quantum or Energy-based ones.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## **5. Turn Sequence**

### **Step 1: Initialization Phase**

-   Clear temporary effects and cooldowns.
-   Regenerate energy based on active systems.

### **Step 2: Equipment Phase**

-   Install or flip cards between sides.
-   Maintain no more than 6 Equipment cards active.

### **Step 3: Action Phase**

-   Declare and resolve all Action-side effects.
-   Attacks specify **energy type** and **severity**.

### **Step 4: Defense Phase**

-   Apply defense modifiers from active equipment.
-   Step down wound severity per defense level.

### **Step 5: Resolution Phase**

-   Fill wound slots for final severity.
-   Destroy or disable damaged equipment as required.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## **6. Example Exchange**

**Street Samurai (Cybernetic)** vs **Quizard (Quantum)**

**Samurai Setup:** - Reflex Boosters (+1 Kinetic Defense) - Targeting
Eye (Accuracy +1) - Smartgun (Attack 6, Kinetic)

**Quizard Setup:** - Phase Cloak (+1 Field Defense) - Quantum Blade
(Attack 6, Field) - Entropic Core (Amplify Field Attacks)

**Turn 1:** - Samurai attacks with Smartgun (6 Kinetic).\
Quizard's Field Defense does not apply → takes full 6 wounds. - Quizard
retaliates with Quantum Blade (6 Field).\
Samurai's Kinetic Defense irrelevant → takes full 6 wounds.

**Turn 2:** - Samurai flips Targeting Eye into Action Side → fires
Precision Burst (3 Kinetic, ignores one defense layer).\
- Quizard flips Phase Cloak to Attack → Quantum Spike (10 Field).\
Samurai's Reflexes reduce 10 → 6 → 3 → fills 3 wounds.\
Samurai nearly incapacitated, but all deterministic and predictable.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## **7. Victory Conditions**

-   A player is incapacitated when all wound slots are filled.
-   Alternatively, a deck can specify custom victory conditions (e.g.,
    system shutdown, energy overload, psionic domination).

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## **8. Design Principles**

-   **No randomness.** All effects follow deterministic cause-and-effect
    logic.\
-   **Short matches.** 5--7 turns maximum for typical duels.\
-   **Depth via decisions.** Sequencing and equipment management define
    victory.\
-   **Lore consistency.** Each card represents technology or psionics
    grounded in hard-sci-fi plausibility.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## **9. Future Additions**

-   Team modes with linked energy pools
