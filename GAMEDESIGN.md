**Game Design**

*Premise*
In a technologically advanced world, the player dies after going through a basic tutorial and some game time event only to find out that death isn't permanent but will bring him back to the point just after the end of the tutortial.
The player will retain some skills and abilities but no equipment or other physical items.
The player is a timelooper and needs to increase his skills and abilities to move through the game world which is generated once when a player creates the character to be played.
The world is persistent as it will be reset in the same way as the timelooper.
If the player encounters people during his previous timeloop, these people will be there as well in the next loop.

*Goal*
To defeat the final event which threatens the entire empire.

*Gameplay*
The gameplay is a card based gameplay where cards can be found, stolen, obtained through quests and combat, through interaction with people in the gameworld or game events. There will be more ways as the game development progresses.

The cards come in three variaties:
*Combat cards*
Combat cards are used in combat and only in combat.
*Event cards*
Event cards can be triggered by the player when not in combat.
Event cards can change the flow of time, create things, add abilities, add new game objects to the world or remove or alter them. These event cards can also prevent other events to happen or to lessen or increase severity.
*Progress cards*
Progress cards move the game one time step forwards. These progress cards can trigger random events, introduce new game objects into the world, sway opinions of groups or affect the planned actions o

**The game world**
The game takes place in a highly advanced star empire that has countless planets under control.
The empire is run by an empress and the houses of nobility, the guilds and coorporations.
The player starts as a nobody on a far off planet with no real significance to the Empire.
Each time a player creates a timeloop character, the game world gets populated with events, people and other game objects that are required for the events that the player must overcome in order to win the game.
This state needs to be preserved as the player will end up in that game world each time death resets the timeloop.

**Game rules**
The timeloop character will have
  skills
    skills affect cards and actions in the game world
    passive skills will silently affect outcome of actions that are associated with that skill
    active skills need to be activated by the player and can affect the gameworld outside combat
    knowledge skills are needed to advance attributes beyond a certain points, allow the use of better skills, items and equipment, find better cards, gain more conversational options and choices during events.
  abilities
    abilities are gained through actions and overcoming certain events or situations.
    They affect attributes, cards and almost any aspect of the game. They are game changers and cannot be controlled.
    An ability example:
      After killing 100000 cockroaches, the timelooper gains the Exterminator title, the wielder of this title does twice the amount of damage to cockroaches when in combat. 
  attributes
    For the first stage of the game development
      Physical
      Mental
    For the next stages these will be more detailed
      Physical
        Strength
        Body
        Quickness
      Mental
        Willpower
        Intelligence
        Charisma
  cards
    Combat Cards can either be active (usable during combat), inactive (unusable during combat) or dormant (only usable during combat after a trigger)
    Later in the development decks of cards can be defined.
  inventory
    The inventory holds non-equiped items
  equipment
    Later in the development there will be equipment slots which can hold certain types of equipment
  memories
    Memories from the previous loops to keep track of what has happened so far and help find certain locations and people more easily
  events
    These are the events that are chained together in order for the player to win the game. These will not be shown only to be kept as part of the 
  money
    to buy stuff
  status
    during a loop, status is gained with groups, people and entities. Status can range between kill on sight to legendary
  reknown
    reknown is gained from actions (completing quests, finding things, defeating foes, etc) and is used in general to keep track of celebrity or notorious states.
  affinities
    during the game many different paths open up and some paths are easier to go on than others due to affinities
    One affinity is chosen when creating a timelooper
    Examples
      Hand-to-Hand affinity
      Psionic affinitiy
      Business affinity
      Social affinity
      etc
  assets 
    (cars, ships, houses, businesses, cults, etc)


**Game play**
The gameplay should be in rounds
  Each round will allow a timelooper to 
    move around in the world
    do research
    search an area
    relax
    run a business
    craft items
    learn new skills
    explore a dungeon
    etc

  When completing a round the timelooper can then
    interact at the new location
      (combat, talk with people, introduce new things (business, cult, guild etc))

**Game Engine**
The engine needs to
  - Simulate the world
  - Generate events
  - Generate locations
  - Generate combat opportunities
  - Generate worlds/solar systems
  - Simulate after-effects of events the timelooper triggers
  - Advance technology/culture
  - Simulate economy
  - Simulate politics
  - Simulate culture/society
  - Simulate wars/conflicts/dungeons/threats
  - Simulate important non-playing-characters
  - Making sure that the game remains enjoyable by
    - Catering to the player's gamestyle
    - Make it easier for the player to accomplish goals in the game based on previous actions
    - Allow synergy with already owned skills/items/abilities/playstyle
